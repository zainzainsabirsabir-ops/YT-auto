Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Ink Film Uploader is a private tool that the owner of one independent folklore film channel uses to publish that channel's own videos. Nobody else uses it. This policy explains what it can access and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it can access

Only what the channel owner allows when they sign in:

  • YouTube: upload videos to the owner's channel; manage that channel's videos, captions, playlists and comments; and view YouTube Analytics reports for that channel.
  • Facebook: list the Pages the owner manages; read engagement on the owner's Page; and publish posts, videos and Reels to that Page.

How it uses that data

  • To publish the channel's own films and Shorts, with their thumbnails, captions and publishing times, and to add them to the channel's playlists.
  • To read view counts and analytics for the channel's own videos, so the owner can see which stories viewers enjoy.

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Where it is kept

The app runs only on the owner's own computer. The sign-in keys that let it reach YouTube and Facebook are stored in files on that computer and nowhere else. Video statistics it reads are also kept on that computer, only as long as the owner needs them to plan the channel's films. The app has no servers and no database of its own.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. The app itself is not a website and uses no cookies.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google: remove the app's access at any time on your Google Account's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Facebook: remove the app in your Facebook settings, under Business integrations.

To have any stored data deleted, email the address below. It is deleted within 7 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect information from them.
