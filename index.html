Ink Film Uploader

A private publishing tool for one independent channel that makes short animated films of myths and folktales from around the world, painted in an ink-wash style.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's finished films and Shorts to its own YouTube channel, with their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and publishing times.
  • Adds the films to the channel's own playlists.
  • Reads view counts and YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own videos, so the owner can see which stories viewers enjoy.
  • Publishes the same films and Shorts as videos and Reels on the channel's own Facebook Page.

Who uses it

Only the channel's owner, on their own computer. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and nobody else can use it.

Your data
